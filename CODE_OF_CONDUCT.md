# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Respecting differing viewpoints and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the community and learners
- Showing empathy toward other community members

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discriminatory language
- Publishing private information without explicit permission
- Sexualized language or imagery in project spaces
- Repeated disruption of discussions or maintainers' work

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards of acceptable behavior. Maintainers may remove comments, commits, issues, pull requests, or other contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct.

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported privately to the maintainers. Reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.
